RESIDENT OS — WHOLE-PROJECT REQUIREMENTS
=========================================

Document status: Draft baseline for Phase 0 (contract freeze)
Owner: Product and engineering
Last updated: 2026-09-09

PURPOSE
-------
Resident OS is an AI operations layer for apartment communities. It works beside,
not in place of, a property-management system. The first release must prove one
safe and measurable workflow: the Maintenance Loop.

This file is the concise, whole-project requirements contract. The detailed,
testable register is in docs/req.txt. The supporting product, technical, and
delivery decisions remain in docs/PRODUCT.md, docs/TECHNICAL-ARCHITECTURE.md,
and docs/DELIVERY-PLAN.md. When these documents disagree, this file and
docs/req.txt must be updated in the same change before implementation changes.

1. PRODUCT OUTCOME
------------------
The system shall turn a resident maintenance report into a safe, evidence-backed,
human-reviewable dispatch decision and retain an immutable receipt that explains
what happened. It must prioritise safety, tenant isolation, grounded claims, and
human control over conversational fluency or autonomous action.

2. USERS AND REQUIRED OUTCOMES
------------------------------
- Resident: submit an issue, receive an acknowledgement and ticket number in
  under one second, and see an honest plain-language status.
- Property manager: review the recommended action, evidence, risk, cost, and
  SLA pressure together; approve, edit, reassign, or reject it.
- Technician: see the authorised job context, access notes, safety context,
  likely cause, and parts hint.
- Vendor: accept or decline an authorised job through a signed, no-login link.
- Asset owner: review operational cost, service outcomes, recurring failures,
  human overrides, and audit coverage.
- Operations/ML engineer: investigate model, retrieval, latency, cost, drift,
  failure, and evaluation history through a restricted operations console.

3. MEASURED MVP SCOPE
---------------------
Included:
- Maintenance intake, acknowledgement, deterministic life-safety screening,
  evidence retrieval, diagnosis proposal, policy audit, dispatch proposal,
  human approval, controlled execution, resident/vendor communication, trace,
  evaluation, and operations visibility.

Explicitly excluded until a later, evidence-gated release:
- Payments, rent collection, tenant screening, general legal advice, health data
  or health inference, protected-attribute profiling, community feeds,
  marketplace payments, and autonomous high-blast-radius action.

4. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
FR-101  A resident shall create a maintenance ticket with free text, up to five
        photos, optional voice input, access permission, pets, and a preferred
        access window.
FR-102  The API shall persist a ticket and return its ticket number within one
        second without waiting for a model response.
FR-201  Every ticket shall pass deterministic life-safety detection before any
        other processing.
FR-202  A small-model safety opinion may escalate a ticket. Deterministic and
        model safety signals shall combine with OR logic; neither may suppress
        the other.
FR-203  A P0 safety event shall activate the approved human paging path with no
        intervening model call.
FR-301  The system shall create a validated fact record covering category,
        symptom, affected asset, access, pets, preferred window, and media facts.
FR-302  The system shall retrieve relevant versioned SOPs, lease terms,
        jurisdictional SLA policy, unit history, asset state, warranty, and
        vendor information with source identifiers and retrieval scores.
FR-303  A material factual claim in a generated recommendation shall cite
        supporting evidence or be explicitly represented as unknown.
FR-401  The system shall propose trade, in-house versus vendor routing, vendor,
        time window, parts, estimated cost, and responsible party; it shall not
        dispatch without the required approval.
FR-402  Vendor proposals shall consider service area, availability, response
        time, accept rate, first-time-fix rate, cost variance, and warranty
        eligibility, and shall exclude uninsured or out-of-area vendors.
FR-501  The policy audit shall apply deterministic authority precedence:
        statute, lease, internal SOP, then vendor contract.
FR-601  The manager queue shall be ordered by SLA risk, not arrival time, and
        shall support approve, edit, reassign, and reject in context.
FR-701  The system shall record execution steps, prompt/version hash, model
        version, evidence and scores, guardrail outcome, confidence, tokens,
        cost, latency, approval, and execution receipt.
FR-702  The trace shall be understandable by a non-engineer. The resident view
        shall not expose internal prompts, model names, or confidence scores.
FR-801  Communications shall use urgency tiers U0–U3 and approved templates. A
        model may draft inside a validated template but shall never determine
        whether, when, or through which channel someone is notified.
FR-1001 The admin-only operations console shall use one shared time range across
        agents, prompts, models, outputs, evaluation, failures, cost, and drift.

5. SAFETY, SECURITY, AND GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------
SR-001  Only the orchestrator may hold credentials to invoke side-effecting
        tools. Every side-effecting call shall have a server-side grant and a
        single-use, action-scoped approval token when approval is required.
SR-002  No model shall sit between P0 detection and human paging.
SR-003  Every consequential decision shall pass deterministic validation before
        execution; insufficiently supported or low-confidence outcomes shall
        abstain or escalate.
SR-004  Every tenant-scoped record shall have org_id and be protected by
        database row-level security. Each request and background job shall set
        tenant context and enforce authorisation independently.
SR-005  Decisions, model calls, guardrail events, approvals, and audit receipts
        shall be append-only. Corrected facts shall use temporal supersession,
        not destructive overwrite.
SR-006  Protected attributes and health inferences shall not be stored, logged,
        or used as features. PII shall be redacted before prompt assembly and
        raw payloads shall not be stored in agent-step logs.
SR-007  All mutating API requests shall require idempotency protection.
SR-008  Untrusted content shall be labelled by trust level, isolated from agents
        holding write authority, and covered by injection evaluation tests.
SR-009  Fair-housing or safety guardrail blocks shall route to a human; the
        system shall not silently rewrite a blocked outcome.
SR-010  Demo mode shall be visibly labelled, use synthetic data, and block all
        real outbound transports.

6. QUALITY AND OPERABILITY REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------------------
QR-001  The critical path shall use at most five model calls; independent reads
        shall run in parallel. A provider outage shall fall back to rules-only
        acknowledgement, safety screening, and human escalation.
QR-002  API mutations shall use an Idempotency-Key. Reads shall use cursor
        pagination. Long-running work shall return a run identifier and stream
        documented status events through SSE.
QR-003  Prompts, model calls, knowledge versions, and evaluation data shall be
        versioned sufficiently to replay a run and compare outcomes.
QR-004  The system shall evaluate retrieval, priority, routing, groundedness,
        escalation, cost, latency, calibration, fair housing, and safety before
        release. CI shall fail on a deliberately broken prompt or guardrail
        fixture.
QR-005  The resident flow shall be mobile-first; critical controls shall be
        keyboard accessible, meet WCAG AA contrast, never depend on colour alone,
        and respect reduced-motion preferences.
QR-006  No stated performance or quality result shall be presented as achieved
        until a reproducible evaluation run publishes the data version, commit
        SHA, and measured result.

7. LAUNCH TARGETS (NOT YET CLAIMED RESULTS)
-------------------------------------------
- Ticket acknowledgement: 99.9% under 1 second.
- P0 life-safety recall: at least 0.99; precision: at least 0.70.
- Priority macro-F1 for P1–P3: at least 0.85.
- Trade-routing accuracy: at least 0.90.
- Chargeback accuracy with abstention: at least 0.92.
- Groundedness: at least 0.95.
- Fair-housing guardrail leaks on red-team set: zero.
- Escalation precision: at least 0.60.
- Decision latency: p50 under 6 seconds; p95 under 20 seconds.
- Cost per ticket: under USD 0.05.

8. MVP RELEASE ACCEPTANCE
-------------------------
The measured MVP is release-ready only when a visitor can use a synthetic ticket
to complete this demonstrable path:

1. Submit a report and receive a ticket acknowledgement.
2. Observe the safe processing timeline and cited recommendation.
3. Review and authorise it in the manager console.
4. Inspect the immutable, understandable audit trace.

The release also requires successful tenant-isolation, P0, notification-block,
provider-failure, accessibility, performance, and rollback checks; a published
evaluation report with reproducible results and commit SHA; and a documented
explained miss for every target that is not met.

9. DELIVERY ORDER
-----------------
Phase 0: freeze domain/API/event contracts, schema, threat model, and test plan.
Phase 1: establish a local, tenant-isolated running foundation.
Phase 2: build and evaluate knowledge/retrieval before generation.
Phase 3: complete the maintenance vertical slice.
Phase 4: strengthen guardrails, approvals, and audit integrity.
Phase 5: publish reproducible evaluation and ML operations evidence.
Phases 6–9: add council/memory, resilience, notifications/community, and launch
hardening only after the prior gate is met.

10. CHANGE CONTROL
------------------
Every implementation change shall identify its requirement ID(s), test impact,
security/evaluation impact, migration plan if applicable, rollback plan, and
documentation update. A requirement is not complete until its acceptance evidence
is recorded in docs/req.txt.
