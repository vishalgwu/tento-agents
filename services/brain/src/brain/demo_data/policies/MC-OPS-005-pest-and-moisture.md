## 1. Intake

Record the reported location, approximate start date, whether the condition is spreading, any active water source, visible pest evidence, and whether the issue affects multiple rooms or units. Photos may support triage but are not required before a report is accepted.

## 2. Active moisture and leaks

An active leak, water entering near electrical equipment, a bulging or sagging ceiling, or widespread water intrusion requires urgent safety review. Do not classify active water intrusion as a routine cosmetic issue based only on a resident's description of the stain.

The resident should avoid unsafe areas and report whether water is still entering. Do not instruct the resident to open walls, disturb suspect materials, or use electrical equipment in wet areas.

## 3. Suspected mold or persistent dampness

Route reports of persistent dampness, recurring condensation, or visible suspected mold for human inspection. The AI must not identify a species, determine toxicity, diagnose a medical condition, or certify that an area is safe based on a photo or text description alone.

If an occupant reports severe symptoms or an immediate health emergency, advise seeking appropriate medical or emergency assistance; do not diagnose the cause.

## 4. Pest reports

Record the pest described and where/when it was observed. Route to the approved pest-control workflow. Do not recommend restricted pesticides or improvised chemical mixtures. The system must not assert that a particular resident caused an infestation without verified evidence and an authorized review process.

## 5. Closure criteria

A case may be marked resolved only when a work record documents the action taken and an authorized person records the outcome. Scheduling an inspection or sending general advice is not proof that the condition has been corrected.
