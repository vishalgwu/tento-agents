## 1. Maintenance reporting

Residents should report defects promptly through the resident portal or the property's published maintenance contact. Reports should identify the affected room or common area, describe visible symptoms, state whether the problem is ongoing, and mention any immediate safety concern. Residents should not delay reporting a serious leak, electrical hazard, loss of secure entry, or other potential emergency while gathering photos.

## 2. Access coordination

For nonemergency work, staff should follow the property's verified notice and access procedure, document the appointment window, and identify the authorized worker. Emergency entry rules must come from the actual lease and applicable law; this synthetic policy does not establish a legal right of entry or a notice period.

If access cannot be confirmed, the system should record the failed coordination attempt and request human follow-up. It must not mark a job complete solely because a visit was scheduled.

## 3. Resident safety and permitted actions

Residents may move clear of a hazard, keep children and pets away from a work area, and provide safe access when feasible. Residents must not be asked to remove electrical covers, manipulate gas equipment, climb onto roofs, enter confined spaces, or undertake work outside ordinary safe household actions.

## 4. Preventive maintenance

The fictional property may schedule inspections of visible plumbing fixtures, HVAC filters, door hardware, smoke/CO alarm status, and common-area conditions. Staff must follow equipment instructions and approved site procedures. The system may recommend inspection but may not claim an inspection occurred unless a dated work record confirms it.

## 5. Resident-caused damage and charges

This document does not establish that a resident caused damage or owes money. Any proposed charge requires documented evidence, the applicable lease provision, required review, and authorization outside this policy. Do not infer fault from the existence of damage or from the resident who submitted the report.
