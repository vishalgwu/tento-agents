## Narrow source observations to verify before use

- Maryland Real Property §8-211 addresses rent-escrow and habitability
  proceedings for specified serious conditions.
- Its timing analysis is tied to notice, condition, and the facts of the case;
  the statute uses a reasonable-time concept rather than creating a universal
  30-day maintenance promise.
- The source includes a rebuttable presumption related to a condition not being
  corrected after more than 30 days. That is not an internal dispatch target and
  must be interpreted by counsel in its complete statutory context.
