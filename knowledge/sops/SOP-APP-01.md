# Temporary appliance-service fixture

> This is evaluation content. It is not a service SLA, warranty statement,
> procurement rule, or approval to repair, replace, reimburse, or dispatch.

## Accountable role

The Appliance Program Owner must provide the equipment-specific, warranty, and
vendor-approved replacement source before production use. A retrieval result
may help a manager organize reported facts, but it does not determine whether
an appliance is covered, repairable, or eligible for replacement.

## Temporary intake record

Capture the reported appliance, symptom, unit/location reference, prior
authorized service record, and any manufacturer information already held in a
property-approved system. Do not instruct residents to disassemble equipment,
work with utility connections, or perform diagnostics.

## Human decision points

An authorized manager considers verified technician findings, approved budget
authority, documented lease obligations, applicable policy, and vendor terms.
The system must expose those citations and any uncertainty; it must not
auto-approve a repair or replacement, issue a purchase request, or contact a
vendor.

## Unknowns and conflicts

If the appliance model, service history, lease responsibility, warranty, or
vendor agreement is absent, say so. Request the accountable owner’s verified
source rather than applying an invented age, cost, or response-time threshold.
