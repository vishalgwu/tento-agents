# Temporary community-rules fixture

> This synthetic document is a retrieval and citation fixture. It is not a
> resident handbook, lease addendum, enforcement rule, or authorization to
> issue notices, fines, access restrictions, or adverse actions.

## Intended use

The future production corpus will contain property-specific rules approved by
the Community Operations Owner and, where required, legal review. This fixture
only demonstrates the difference between explanatory context and enforceable
source material.

## Temporary topic taxonomy

Use these labels only to classify a report for human review:

- quiet enjoyment and noise reports;
- common-area cleanliness and safety concerns;
- parking, pets, guests, and shared-facility questions; and
- emergency-access or egress concerns.

Do not infer a violation from a report or invent exact limits, penalties,
curfews, guest counts, or exception rules. The system must preserve the report,
retrieve a verified rule when available, and let the authorized policy owner
decide the next step.

## Promotion requirement

Before production use, the Community Operations Owner must supply the current
property-specific policy, effective window, enforcement process, resident
notice version, and required review/approval record. A temporary fixture may
never be promoted merely because it resembles common community rules.
